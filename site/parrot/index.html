Instrument · Singing synth

Parrot

Type the words, play the notes, and it sings them back.

Each MIDI note sings the next syllable of the lyrics, with a formant voice built from scratch: no samples, no recordings.

How it works

Words split into syllables by themselves, shown as chips under the lyrics. When one comes out wrong, split it with hyphens (par-rot) or spell it the way it sounds (lay-zee). A + on its own holds the last vowel over another note.

In On the beat timing, consonants start up to 150 ms early so the vowel lands on the note, as a singer's would; the host compensates for the delay. Live plays straight away, for playing a keyboard.

The lyrics start over whenever the transport starts. Click a chip, or use the arrow buttons or CC50 and CC51, to move by hand.

Controls

  1. SizeFrom a small voice to a big one.
  2. ToneSoft and breathy to bright and pressed.
  3. Breath and DictionAir in the voice, and how hard the consonants hit.
  4. Vibrato, Rate and GlideVibrato comes in after the note starts; the mod wheel adds more.
  5. Release, Lo-Fi and VolumeLo-Fi crushes it toward an old speech chip.
