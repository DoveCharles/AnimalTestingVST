Effect · Maximizer

Good Boy

One-knob multiband maximizer. Based on a certain DAW’s signature effect.

Turn up Amount and it gets louder and denser. At zero it leaves the sound alone. Mode picks how it gets there: warm, bright, loud or big.

How it works

Linkwitz-Riley crossovers split the sound into low, mid and high bands that add back up flat. Each mode moves the split points and sets each band's compressor.

Each band has a stereo-linked compressor with its own makeup gain. The bands are summed, lightly saturated, and passed to a lookahead limiter that holds peaks at -0.3 dBFS. The meters show each band's gain reduction and the limiter's.

Signal path

  1. Three-band splitLinkwitz-Riley crossovers.
  2. Band compressorsStereo-linked, with makeup.
  3. WidthExtra side signal on the high band.
  4. SaturationSoft, on the sum.
  5. Limiter1.5 ms lookahead, -0.3 dBFS.
