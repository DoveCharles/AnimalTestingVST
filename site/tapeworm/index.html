Tapeworm

Worn tape emulator with speed controls: slow the tape itself down to a quarter of real time, live.

Fitted to measurements of real tape processing. The Speed dial slows the tape itself, down to 0.25x, for up to five minutes of tape. Drive it, wobble it, wear it out, or stop it in time with the song. The reels are apples, and the tape is a worm.

How it works

Wow, flutter, wear and the tape stop all move the read point of one delay line, the way a real transport moves tape past the head, so pitch and timing shift together.

The reels turn with the tape. They slow down with Speed and Tape Stop and follow the host's transport.

Signal path

  1. DriveA symmetric two-stage soft clipper, 4x oversampled.
  2. NoiseMono hiss.
  3. ToneThe tape voicing.
  4. WearLevel flutter and speed jitter.
  5. TransportWow, flutter, Tape Stop and Speed.
  6. FilterLow-pass or high-pass.
  7. Mechanical noiseLooped motor rumble, after the voicing.
