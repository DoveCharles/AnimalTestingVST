Effect · Overdrive

Blue Heeler

Low-gain blues overdrive with two cascaded clipping stages.

Made to sit at the end of the chain, after reverb and delay. Its bias sits slightly off centre, adding the even harmonics that make it sound like an amp rather than a pedal.

How it works

Stage one clips softly through diodes in its feedback loop. It's logarithmic with no hard ceiling, so picking dynamics survive.

Stage two runs into the rails of its discrete op-amp, which rounds off the peaks and gives the amp-like compression once the gain is up.
